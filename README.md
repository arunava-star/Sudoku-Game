# Sudoku-Game

A fully responsive and interactive Sudoku game built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Play Sudoku with three difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard), check your answers, and enjoy a celebratory animation when you win.

Features:

Three Difficulty Levels: Choose from Easy, Medium, or Hard to generate Sudoku puzzles.

Interactive Gameplay: Fill in the grid with numbers and validate your solution.

Check Answer: Verify if your solution is correct.

Solve Puzzle: Automatically solve the puzzle with one click.
 
Timer: Track your solving time.

Winning Celebration: Enjoy a fun animation when you solve the puzzle correctly.

Fully Responsive: Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
